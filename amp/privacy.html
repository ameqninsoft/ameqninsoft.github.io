AMP – AMEQNIN Music Player

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 21 September 2026

In short. AMP plays the music files that are already on your phone. It has no account and no server of its own. Your library, playlists and favourites stay on your device. The app only contacts a few third-party services to find song lyrics, and it shows ads through Google AdMob. Details below.

1. Who is responsible

The app “AMP – AMEQNIN Music Player” (package com.ameqninsoft.amp) is published by the individual developer AMEQNIN, based in Germany.
Contact for anything related to privacy: ameqninsoft@gmail.com

2. What stays on your device

The following is processed and stored only on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • the list of audio files found on your device (title, artist, album, genre, duration, file name and path);
  • cover art (the one embedded in your files and any cover you pick yourself from your files or photos);
  • your playlists, favourites, listening statistics, saved A-B loops, equalizer presets and app settings;
  • the last song and position, and the playback queue, so playback can resume.

Your music files themselves are never uploaded. If you edit ID3 tags or a cover, the change is written to the file on your device after you approve the system prompt.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy AMP needs it
Music and audio (read)To find and play the songs stored on your phone. Required.
NotificationsTo show playback controls in the notification, on the lock screen and with Bluetooth or headset buttons. Optional.
MicrophoneOnly to animate the music visualizer with the sound being played. Nothing is recorded or stored. Optional.
InternetTo look up lyrics (section 4) and to display ads (section 5).

You can change these permissions at any time in Android Settings › Apps › AMP.

4. Requests sent to third-party services

To find lyrics, AMP sends short text queries to the services below. AMP does not download album covers from the internet. Like any internet request, they also receive your IP address and the app's user-agent (AMEQNIN-Music-Player-android/1.0). We do not send your files, your playlists or any account identifier, because we do not have any.

ServicePurposeData sent
LRCLIB
lrclib.net		 Find lyrics when you open the lyrics screen Title, artist, album and duration of the song
lyrics.ovh
lyrics.ovh		 Fallback source for plain-text lyrics Artist and title of the song
You are in control. These lookups can be turned off at any time with the Online lookups switch in the app (Tune menu). When it is off, AMP sends none of the requests in this section.

Covers you pick yourself (from your files or photos) stay on your device: they are written into the file's tag after you approve the system prompt, or kept in AMP's private storage.

5. Advertising

AMP displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may process data such as your IP address, device and app information, and your Android advertising ID, according to Google’s advertising policies and Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ads personalisation in Android Settings › Google › Ads.

6. Google Play services

If you choose “Rate this app”, the review dialog is provided by Google Play. Google processes the data related to it under its own Privacy Policy. If you install AMP from Google Play, Google also handles the installation and update data.

7. Backups

Depending on your device settings, Android may back up the app’s data (for example playlists and settings) to your Google account (Android Auto Backup) and restore it when you set up a new device. This is handled by Google and your device, not by us.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep no personal data about you on any server. Data stored by the app (section 2) is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings. There is no account to delete.

9. Children

AMP is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age) and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or elsewhere with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most of these requests concern the third parties listed above; you can also write to us at ameqninsoft@gmail.com and we will help as far as we can.

11. Security

All requests to the services listed above use HTTPS. Data stored on your device is protected by Android’s app sandbox.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes (for example a new service is used), this page is updated and the date at the top is changed. The current version is always available at this address.
