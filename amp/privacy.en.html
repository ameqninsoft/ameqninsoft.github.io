AMP – AMEQNIN Music Player

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 23 September 2026

In short. AMP plays the music files that are already on your phone. It has no account and no server of its own. Your library, playlists and favourites stay on your device. AMP can also send music from one of your phones to another one directly, without any server. The app only contacts a few third-party services to find song lyrics, and it shows ads through Google AdMob. Details below.

2. What stays on your device

The following is processed and stored only on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • the list of audio files found on your device (title, artist, album, genre, duration, file name and path);
  • cover art (the one embedded in your files and any cover you pick yourself from your files or photos);
  • your playlists, favourites, listening statistics, saved A-B loops, equalizer presets and app settings;
  • the last song and position, and the playback queue, so playback can resume.

Your music files themselves are never uploaded to us or to any third party. They only leave your phone if you use the phone-to-phone transfer (section 4), and then only to the phone you choose. If you edit ID3 tags or a cover, the change is written to the file on your device after you approve the system prompt.

4. Transfer between two phones

AMP can send music and app data from one phone to another phone that also runs AMP (Tune › Transfer). This feature is optional and only works when you start it on both phones.

  • Direct link, no server. The two phones talk to each other over Wi-Fi Direct or over the same Wi-Fi network. Nothing goes through the Internet or through a server of ours, and we receive nothing.
  • What can be sent. The albums you choose and, if you tick them, your playlists, favourites, listening statistics, saved loops, the covers you chose, and your settings (theme, background, language, audio, equalizer, exclusions).
  • What the other phone sees. The model name of your phone (for example “moto x4”) while the two phones connect.
  • You stay in control. A 6-digit pairing code shown on the receiving phone must be typed on the sending phone. The receiving phone shows what is coming and the space it needs, and asks for approval before anything is received. The sender’s settings are applied only if the receiver leaves that option ticked. Every file is verified after transfer.
  • Security of the link. Wi-Fi Direct is encrypted by Android. On the same Wi-Fi network AMP does not add encryption of its own, so use a network you trust.
  • What is kept. Received songs are stored in Music › AMP Transfers on the receiving phone. Received playlists, favourites, statistics and loops are merged with the ones already on that phone; received settings replace its own settings. A small record of a partly received file is kept only so an interrupted transfer can resume, and is removed when the file is complete.

Covers you pick yourself (from your files or photos) stay on your device: they are written into the file's tag after you approve the system prompt, or kept in AMP's private storage.

6. Advertising

AMP displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may process data such as your IP address, device and app information, and your Android advertising ID, according to Google’s advertising policies and Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ads personalisation in Android Settings › Google › Ads.

7. Google Play services

If you choose “Rate this app”, the review dialog is provided by Google Play. Google processes the data related to it under its own Privacy Policy. If you install AMP from Google Play, Google also handles the installation and update data.

8. Backups

Depending on your device settings, Android may back up the app’s data (for example playlists and settings) to your Google account (Android Auto Backup) and restore it when you set up a new device. This is handled by Google and your device, not by us.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep no personal data about you on any server. Data stored by the app (section 2) is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings. There is no account to delete. Songs received through the phone-to-phone transfer (section 4) are ordinary files in your Music folder: they stay there after uninstalling AMP until you delete them yourself.

10. Children

AMP is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age) and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or elsewhere with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most of these requests concern the third parties listed above; you can also write to us at ameqninsoft@gmail.com and we will help as far as we can.

12. Security

All requests to the services listed above use HTTPS. Data stored on your device is protected by Android’s app sandbox. The phone-to-phone transfer (section 4) does not use the Internet: it is protected by the pairing code and, with Wi-Fi Direct, by Android’s own encryption.
